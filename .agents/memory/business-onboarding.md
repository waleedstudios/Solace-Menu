---
name: Business onboarding
description: The user's required setup flow after authentication.
---

After Google sign-in, ask for business name, store address, and business phone before the dashboard. Do not automatically call it the person's first name's restaurant.

For phone-number registration, the user also requires the person's name and email along with business name and store address.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested actual business information after sign-in instead of automatic first-name-based restaurant naming.

**How to apply:** Keep identity sign-in separate from business setup. Returning users with completed setup should retain their business and data rather than receive another automatically generated restaurant.
